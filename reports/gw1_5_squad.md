## Methodology & caveats
Same scoring as the GW1 picker (shrunk per-90 rates × starts reliability, blended points/xGI per position, small per-GW fixture factor 0.85-1.15, set-piece bonus each GW) **except** the xGI→points conversion is a per-position regression `points_per_90 = α + β·xGI` (intercept fitted, minutes-weighted). Calibrated α/β: DEF 3.24/6.41, MID 3.25/4.05, FWD 2.45/5.42 — the baseline α keeps appearance/clean-sheet/bonus points from being scaled up by a player's xGI.
- **Preseason projection:** per-90 rates are last season's; no GW1-5 data exists yet.
- Difficulty is the FPL's official 1-5 rating, not a model — the fixture factor is intentionally small (our research found fixture features weak predictors).
- **Variance:** 339.2 is an expected value. A no-transfer five-week window amplifies injury/rotation/form risk; the model can't foresee those.
- Auto-subs are modelled as: GK replaced by bench GK with probability (1 − starts reliability); outfield misses ~ Poisson(λ), each bench sub contributing when k+ starters are out. The EV is small (+2.6) because starters are reliable.
